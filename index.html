About the Director

Prof. Chao Zhai

Chao Zhai is a Full Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Automation, China University of Geosciences, Wuhan, China[cite: 1]. He earned his Ph.D. in Complex Systems and Control from the Institute of Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences (2013) under the supervision of Prof. Yiguang Hong[cite: 2].

Prior to joining CUG, Prof. Zhai served as a Research Fellow at the Singapore-ETH Centre (ETH Zurich)[cite: 1], a Research Associate at the University of Hong Kong[cite: 1], and a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Bristol (UK) working with Prof. Mario di Bernardo[cite: 1].

Research Areas

Multi-Agent Cooperative Control

Distributed sweep and coverage algorithms in non-convex regions, conformal mapping, load balancing, and UAV fleet optimization for geohazard monitoring[cite: 1, 3, 4].

Resilient Systems & CPS

Cascade failure prevention in power networks, Model Predictive Control (MPC), dynamic security assessment, and Control Barrier Functions (CBFs)[cite: 1].

Active Disturbance Rejection & Motor Coordination

Tracking Differentiator (TD) theory, ADRC for flight simulation test facilities, and interactive virtual player algorithms for social motor coordination[cite: 1, 3, 4, 7, 8].
