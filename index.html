About Professor Chao Zhai

Prof. Chao Zhai is a Full Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Automation, China University of Geosciences (Wuhan). He received his Ph.D. in Complex Systems and Control from the Institute of Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences (2013). Prior to joining CUG, he held research positions at the Singapore-ETH Centre (ETH Zurich), University of Hong Kong, and University of Bristol.

IEEE Senior Member H-index: 25 Google Citations: 1800+

Research Focus

Cooperative & Coverage Control

Distributed coverage algorithms, non-convex region partitioning, multi-agent sweep coverage, and geohazard monitoring systems.

Resilient Systems & CPS

Cascade failure prevention in power grids, Model Predictive Control (MPC), Control Barrier Functions (CBFs), and game theory.

Active Disturbance Rejection & Motor Coordination

Tracking Differentiators (TD), ADRC for flight environment simulation, and interpersonal social motor coordination in joint actions.
